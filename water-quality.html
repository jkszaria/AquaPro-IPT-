Our Water Quality Promise

At AquaPro, we are committed to providing the safest, cleanest drinking water to every household in Catarman, Northern Samar. Our water undergoes rigorous filtration and regular testing to ensure it meets the highest standards.

Our Filtration Process

Every drop goes through a 4-stage purification system

Sediment Filtration

Removes large particles like sand, dirt, rust, and other suspended solids

Carbon Filtration

Eliminates chlorine, organic compounds, bad taste, and unpleasant odors

Reverse Osmosis

Removes up to 99% of dissolved salts, bacteria, and heavy metals

UV Sterilization

Eliminates any remaining microorganisms for absolute purity
